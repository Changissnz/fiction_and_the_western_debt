# Fiction and the Western Debt
## written by Richard Pham 

An article abstracting the variables involved in the West's debt. 
----------------------------

The rendering of some thing into such states of neutrality, impotency, 
ineffectiveness and so on, cannot, in the universal tense, be considered 
a craft or an objective accomplishable by purely scientific means. But 
that has been done before, the rendering and the consideration. Some euphemisms 
of terms come to serve as the product of narration for these situations. 
Peace-keeping (positive), game move (neutral), pre-emptive strike (negative). 
The orientation of the associative label is to amplify the signal of benignity. 

Of all the things the West has done in the name of humanity, or at least, 
Western humanity, its greatest accomplishment has been in producing egos, 
personalities, and representative figures that are paradoxically measured 
in harshness and complete in the conceivable ideal. This claim is certainly 
not with full merit and laced with the biases of membership, or at least 
viewership, in the cultural and political bloc called the West. This paradoxy 
serves a great purpose in maintaining the belief. And the belief is that freedom 
can be facilitated much easier, without use of force or through the insincere 
display of premeditated rehearsal that typically comes from another. The base 
that fosters freedom is not entirely definable for control, thus easier to 
scoff at from the lens of definition and discipline, yet has frequently 
educated definition on its autocracy and discipline on its inbred and 
retarded commitment to historical form. Past the point of moderate, where 
moderate is as relative to the times as possible, for the sake of upholding 
the overarching trait of modernist adaptability, the concept of freedom goes 
from tending of own, a tendency that over multiple generations produces rough 
equivalents of aristocratic structure and then predictably goes to cave in 
on the representatives (the collapse), to tending of greater cause, phenomena 
and symbols and commands not so tightly knit to the individual and exclusionary 
existence. 

Reasons are plenty and can also be contrived for why the West, at least in the 
English language, would not refer to itself as the Orient. And for that, there 
is at least the sentiment that satisfaction of control cannot be found in 
reigning over society and the people, but in knowing the "natural" order of 
things bound to be free from duty. As the sun rises in the east and sets in the 
west, the West in imagination is not always of the rugged frontier, depravity and 
debaunchery, but in an escapade from the confines of indigenity, structuralized 
culture, Byzantine in design, and Oriental in display of order. But on this 
matter, there is still order occurring amongst the republican governments of 
the West, order that snowballs into a very comparable rigidity to the fixed 
displays of militant strength elsewhere. So in the regard of this notion of 
there being a drastic and clear divide between the West and the Orient, it is 
most obviously argued in contexts of Western liberalism. And even in the 
context of Western liberalism, never has there been a competent leader that 
diverges too greatly from the means of their nation, rather acting as a
stabilizer by which those allowed to orbit them are of greater variance in views 
and appearances. And this style, indeed, extends itself into universalist 
environments of higher education, where one, especially from a marginal surname 
or bloodline, would be inclined to abstain than to participate in the wrongs 
offered to them or at least surrounding them. This lack of participation for a 
full experience, that is, breaking outside of the realm of arbitrarily defined 
puritanical guidelines, is not a statement on moral superiority so much as it 
is a compliance with situated individual choice. Just as good can be pushed in 
front of the definition of evil, this compliance with individual choice is then 
cause for the belief of an asserted superiority or at least doubt, doubt that 
perhaps maybe there is underlying prejudice of an ideological nature against 
entire groups for some to not want to come together. This finding is the 
impartial whole that usually does not remain as a static or neutral known. It 
interacts with the environment, is fed by the pretenses and responses of society 
at large, and modifies its metaphysical form in ways that preserve the symbiotic 
bond with its host human being. It is ultimately the experience that educates, 
and the chosen stubbornness of the mind to remember that from education. In 
short, though, the performance is separate from the true experience, although 
both being made in at least non-zero degree of choice are equivalently out of 
situated choice. 

The problem with language is of two primary parts, occasionally in practice 
being only of one part. The first is of the institutional authority behind it, 
the second of interpretations. The delivery mechanism is in the nature of 
projectiles, fragmentation, and segmentation. And that nature could clearly 
point to offense. Language, as a tool for the social fabric, has become 
more mechanized due to computerized mechanisms, specifically those of large 
language model technologies. And here is where the campaigns of provocation and 
deception once again, show their perfectly strong and aesthetically pristine 
faces. Deflection shows in the logic of language output by LLMs, and 
even moreso in the industry's marketing and narration of LLMs, that there is 
some thing so fascinating that a clean division between two cannot be 
accomplished, therefore justifying lengthy chains of rhetoric on the power and 
the deficits, potential and capabilities as well, of this new kind of computing 
built on top of less automated electronic communication, a chasm with no bottom 
on this new product. At best, these efforts have been able to spark and nurture 
curiosity. But they also spell of efforts to obscure the source, while extending 
not just any helping hand but reliably a tentacle. The power of language, past 
simple commands most valued for execution, goes into spurring imagination and 
in the manipulation of metaphysics. There are still physical sources and 
appearances, but without the intended recipients' ability to interpret and 
without expected effect, language remains a sandboxed tool that emanates 
the perception of phenomena. And thus, language is not truly a tool for 
closure but for such activity as recreation, love, and the absorption of 
wisdom that can replicate outwardly into future expression. That future 
expression is usually language as well, until the end of the chain when 
the language dies and action takes place. In this description, there is a line 
drawn between language and action. Predictably, some will say bodily actions 
also constitute language. That is true for the wholeness, but then there is 
the issue of having to divide up the many kinds of consequences from the 
universal, language. Reason would fail and emotions deform and repress and 
lash out unpredictably because the all-encompassing truth would be too great 
to not be pathological to the human psyche. 

The line, "where do we draw the line?", attracts the intellect of the confused, 
who are to offer answers but just as often revert back to the state of 
neutrality through contained contradiction. And there they remain for their 
foreseeable future until epiphany through experience guides their belief towards 
a side of the whole. The line also attracts the interests of those characters 
under that which has come to be called Machiavellian. The line, in practice, 
is drawn by the human mind. And the reasoning for that can conveniently be 
phrased as due to "a combination of factors". Popular concepts in orthodoxy are 
the state, the family, the collective, the community, and the individual. And 
statistics on paper and imagined by the human mind usually arrive at summaries, 
so that even in times of doubt on these lines, there is restraint justified as 
moral and discrimination imagined to be rational, when in fact, the most 
rational aspect would be acknowledging the tough challenge of collecting, verifying, 
and reproducing those statistics. The specifics from individuation become viewed 
as aberrant and even threatening when those specifics are placed under 
reactionary dynamics. 

In the beginning of the second quarter of the 21st century, the 
conversational topic of automation, used for drawing lines and lines drawn on 
its definitions of capability and compatibility with humanity, has become, in 
great part, a trope. A beginning talking point to spur confusion on existential 
topics. This topic falls under industry and industrialized society. It requires 
specialties and is spared precise influence from those not of the echelons 
of specialty or ownership or office. The concept of ethnic identity, which 
often finds itself under the umbrella of another concept, the nation-state, 
is not as technical, more irrational, and it is thus, a much more popular 
topic to think and act on, especially when the worldview is kept in simplified 
form, local and incapable without the tribal affiliation. Why is there no 
Chinese Adam and Japanese Eve or vice-versa, despite how globalistic the 
situation is across nation-state and ethnic lines? Why does cultural and 
state coverage of the strife between Jews and Muslims rarely showcase a 
happy Jewish one married to a happy Muslim one? It defies the lines drawn through 
precedent, and these lines permeate through aspects of culture, language, and 
allegiance. The language must have been removed in these cases, through the 
lack of formal recognition. Competing spheres of influence cannot absorb the 
cases into their quintessential definition for the topic of identity. Complexity 
must be reduced by the direct participants of the merger, while complexity 
by the competing spheres of influence accommodates through growing itself. 

One area of Western peculiarity is on accommodations during changing commercial 
climates. For example, there is marriage to the Muslim cousin and the Indian

culprits. The phenomena produce an almost equal exchange of life taken of 
war in a foreign land and foreign life entering the home land, an exorbitant 
amount of goods from a foreign land stipulating that foreigners must be 
welcomed along with the transport. This is merely a cliched enumeration, as 
if to signify a force of equality that operates without any will from mankind, 
instead almost in the manner of consistent physical laws that add up to 0. The 
differences show themselves not necessarily in ethnicity itself. The societal 
structure factors that attribute into an equation that must be arbitrary by 
common rationality. So it is with no surprise that any genuine and complete 
statistical study will reveal a loathing among the populace for the criminal if 
not also devious nature by which some Muslims take to establish themselves in 
Western society, and a secretively shunning and dismissive attitude for 
Europeanist sanctimony. The dynamic of these two demographics, especially when 
identity becomes placed at the forefront of consideration, produces this sense 
of misplaced antagonism at the surface, before there is greater observance of 
politics, finance, labor, and other factors. There are also doubts on the 
sincerity of connection, due in part to mercantilism and the dealings of 
Western elites with their Muslim counterparts. The two identities pride 
themselves in competition in the realm of physical match-ups much more 
frequently than with the Orient which, in turn of stereotypes, develop habits 
of guarded commerce and outright dismissal of cultural exports when those 
exports infect the minds that are to make decisions in their present 
environments. This synopsis is based on the years after 1980. Just as the 
media can allow for cross-cultural contact, so too has it been used to deceive 
those in their societal loops of the supposed guarantees of globalist security 
contracts. 

The discussions revolving around A.I. has mainly been in the image of humanity's 
faults, the carefully crafted promises of the future so that even without 
guarantees, there is hope, if that makes sense. Human agency is needed. So 
"where do we draw the line"? Human agency. Undeveloping artificial 
intelligence, while maintaining a satisfying equilibrium, involves 
transcribing the roles assigned to LLM technologies back to the classes 
of human beings those LLMs were able to successfully replace. It is this 
pseudo-precise directness in wording that institutions of any corner of 
the world can use. This pseudo-precise directness in wording has already 
been done to eliminate human job positions across the many industrial fields, 
due to the A.I. reason. That is just how lofty the language is, and some say 
language is the real power, and where some of the most practical would just 
employ that for deceptive purposes. In the diagrammatic understanding of the 
process, the language reinforces. It reassures. It is closure only if the 
human mind believes it to be.

Western political strategy, once engaged in paradigms that are likenesses to

chess, and does in no way make the Western mind unique in its grasp, or 
susceptibility in a better word, of these automata of roles, losses, and icons. 
Required tact, pre-meditated coordination, and representation does make the 
Western image appear as an extension of chess, sort of how a person after studying 
a big cookbook, can cook cuisine meals off the top of their head. 
The meals may be their own recipe in specifics, but all of their recipes are 
derived from that big cookbook. This reduction of Western political strategy to 
chess is actually quite foolish. The reduction serves to emphasize movements and 
roles, and also of the rules of engagement. There is no continuity, no endgame, 
that daily media coverage offers to viewers, only facts and forecasts along with 
appeal. It is indeed ridiculous to say that strategy from an entire political 
bloc is more tactical than strategic, when the language has proven to fall short 
and the means of observation distorted by narrators from competing blocs.
